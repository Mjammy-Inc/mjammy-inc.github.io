Privacy policy

Timelønsapp is published by Kristoffer Bjerregaard Bundgaard. The publisher is the data controller for the information the app processes.

In short: We store your account and your work details on a server so you can get them back on a new phone. We only use them to make the app work. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics tools in the app, and we do not sell your information.

Last updated: 10 October 2026

What the app stores

  • Your account: email address and password. The password is stored encrypted (as a hash) by the sign-in service, so nobody can read it.
  • Your settings: language, country and currency.
  • Your jobs: name and colour, hourly rates, pay period and payout rules, tax percentage and allowance, supplements and your own deductions.
  • Your shifts: date, start and end time, break and an optional note, plus shortcuts for regular shifts.
  • Payslips: the amounts and hours you type in from a payslip, and an optional note. The app does not store pictures or files of your payslip.

We do not ask for your name, address, national ID number or bank account number, and you should not type them into notes or job names.

Your phone also stores your sign-in (so you do not have to sign in every time), your chosen language and a running shift timer. This stays on the phone, except that the sign-in is used to fetch your data.

What the information is used for

To show your shifts, calculate an estimate of your pay and deductions, show the next payout and compare with your payslip. The legal basis is that it is necessary to provide the app to you (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).

Accounts and server data

Your information is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in provider), which is our data processor under a data processing agreement. Access is locked to your account, so other users cannot see your data. Confirmation and password reset emails to your email address are sent through an email provider. Your sign-in can also be used in other MJAMMY apps, but those apps cannot see your information in Timelønsapp unless you have given permission in the app yourself.

Ads and tracking

The app has no ads, no analytics or tracking tools and no third-party error reporting tools. We do not track what you do in the app.

Purchases and notifications

The app has no in-app purchases and sends no notifications.

Your rights, export and deletion

  • View and correct: everything you have stored can be viewed and edited in the app.
  • Export: in Settings you can export all your shifts as a CSV file that you can open in, for example, Excel or Numbers.
  • Delete: in Settings choose "Delete my account". All your jobs, shifts, payslips and settings are then deleted immediately. If you also use other MJAMMY apps with the same sign-in, only the information in Timelønsapp is deleted and your sign-in remains so the other apps keep working. You can always ask us to delete your entire sign-in by writing to us.
  • Other rights: you can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your information and object to processing. Write to kristoffer@bundgaards.net. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (datatilsynet.dk).

We keep your information for as long as you have an account. If you delete the account, the information is removed from the database.

Children

The app is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information about children under 13.

Amounts are estimates

Amounts in the app are calculated estimates based on the figures you entered yourself. They are not a payslip and not tax or financial advice.

If you contact support

If you write to us, we receive your email address and what you send. We use it to reply and follow up on your enquiry, and we delete it when it is no longer needed. Do not send passwords or card details. You can ask for access to, correction of or deletion of your correspondence by writing to kristoffer@bundgaards.net.

Changes

If we change how the app processes information, we update this page and the date at the top, and we tell you in the app if the change is significant.

When you visit this website

These pages are static. We have not added contact forms, cookies, ads or analytics tools to them. The pages are hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages), which processes technical information such as your IP address and browser details to deliver and protect the site. GitHub's privacy statement applies to that processing. Nothing is sent from the app to this website.
