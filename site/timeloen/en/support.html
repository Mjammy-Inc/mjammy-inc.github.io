Help for Hourlock

Hourlock is for anyone with an hourly-paid job: log your shifts quickly, see what you will be paid and when, and check whether your payslip is right. It works with several jobs, several rates and changing pay periods.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the pay in the app not quite match my payslip?

The app shows an estimate. Small differences usually come from rounding, a different monthly allowance or a different tax percentage than the one you entered. Check the job's tax under Settings, and use "Does the payslip match?" under Pay to see the difference in money and hours.

My pay period and payday are not the same every month

Choose "Changing dates" when you create the job, and enter each period with its first day, last day and payday, for example from your employer's pay calendar. Under Settings you can add the next period, and the app can suggest it based on the previous one.

I have several jobs, and only one can use the main tax card

Set your primary job to the main card with a monthly allowance and the others to the secondary card. The app warns you under Settings if more than one job is set to the main card.

I do not get a confirmation email

Check your spam filter and wait a few minutes. If you still do not get the email, write to support.

Export my data

Under Settings choose "Export shifts (CSV)". You can open the file in, for example, Excel or Numbers.

Delete your data

Open Settings and choose "Delete my account". All your jobs, shifts and payslips are then deleted immediately. If you use the same sign-in in other MJAMMY apps, your sign-in remains so those apps keep working. Write to support if you want your entire sign-in deleted.
