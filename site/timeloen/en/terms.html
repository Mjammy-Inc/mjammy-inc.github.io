What the app is

Timelønsapp helps you log working hours, get an estimate of your pay and compare it with your payslip. The app is an aid.

They are not a payslip, not tax or financial advice, and may differ from what your employer pays out. Your payslip and your employer are always the final word. Check your tax card with the tax authority, and contact your employer if you are unsure about your pay.
