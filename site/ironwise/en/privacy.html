Privacy policy

Ironwise is published by Kristoffer Bjerregaard Bundgaard. The publisher is the data controller for the information the app processes.

In short: We store your account, your training and your friends on a server, so you can get them back on a new phone and so your friends can see your workouts. You choose who your friends are. The app has no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and we do not sell your information.

Last updated: 12 November 2026

What the app stores

  • Your account: you sign in with Apple or Google. We receive an email address (with Apple it can be a hidden address if you choose "Hide My Email") and a user ID from them. We never receive your Apple or Google password, and the app has no password of its own. You can connect both Apple and Google to the same profile.
  • Your profile: a username that you choose. Other people can see the username when they search for it. They can never see your email.
  • Your training: your exercises, your logged sets (weight, reps, date and time), your programs, workouts and weekly plan, and your records and streaks, which are calculated from your sets.
  • Friends: friend requests, who your friends are, and who you have blocked.
  • Comments: the comments you write on your friends' workouts and your own.
  • Reports: if you report a user or a comment, we store the reason, your optional explanation and a copy of the comment text, so we can handle the case even if the comment is deleted.
  • Push notifications (optional): if you turn them on, we store your phone's push token and your choices about which notifications you want.

We do not ask for your real name, address, national ID number or health information. Do not write them in comments or your username.

Your phone also stores your sign-in (so you do not have to sign in every time), your language and unit (kg or lbs) and a copy of your most recently fetched training data, so you can log without internet. Sets you log offline stay on the phone until they can be sent. The copy is deleted when you sign out or delete your account.

What your friends can see

Only users you have accepted as friends can see your workouts: exercises, sets, records, your calendar and your comments. Others can only find you by searching your username and can see the username, nothing else. You can remove a friend or block a user at any time. You then no longer see each other's content.

What the information is used for

To show your training, calculate records and progress, show your friends' workouts in your feed and let you comment on each other's workouts. The legal basis is that it is necessary to provide the app to you (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Handling of reports and blocks is done to protect users from harassment and abuse (Article 6(1)(f), legitimate interest).

Accounts and server data

Your information is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in provider), which is our data processor under a data processing agreement. The server is in Stockholm (EU). Access is locked so other users can only see what you have shared with them as friends. Sign-in is through Apple or Google.

Notifications

If you turn on push notifications, we send them from our server through Expo's push service and Apple's notification service (APNs). They can tell a friend about a new comment or friend request and contain the sender's username, never the comment itself. You can turn off each type under You in the app or in the iPhone settings.

Ads and tracking

The app has no ads, no analytics or tracking tools and no third-party error reporting tools. We do not track what you do in the app. The app has no in-app purchases.

User content

You can report a user or a comment and block a user from the three-dot menu. We handle reports within 24 hours and can remove content and close accounts that harass or abuse others. See also the terms.

Your rights, deletion and export

  • View and correct: everything you have stored can be viewed and corrected in the app.
  • Delete your training: under You you can delete all your training history, and under an exercise you can delete the history for that exercise.
  • Delete your account: under You choose "Delete my account". Your profile, exercises, sets, programs, friendships, comments, blocks, reports you sent and your sign-in are then deleted immediately, and your Apple sign-in is revoked. Comments you wrote to others are also removed. This cannot be undone.
  • Export: the app does not have an export button yet. Write to us and we will send your data in a common file format.
  • Other rights: you can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your information and object to processing. Write to kristoffer@bundgaards.net. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (datatilsynet.dk) or your local authority.

We keep your information as long as you have an account. Reports about a user are kept only as long as necessary to handle the case and protect other users.

Children

The app is for people aged 13 or older. We do not knowingly collect information about children under 13.

If you contact support

If you write to us, we receive your email address and what you send. We use it to reply and follow up, and we delete it when it is no longer needed. Do not send passwords or card details.

Changes

If we change how the app handles information, we update this page and the date above, and we say so in the app if the change is significant.

When you visit this website

These pages are static. We have not added contact forms, cookies, ads or analytics to them. The pages are hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages), which processes technical information such as your IP address and browser information to deliver and protect the site. GitHub's privacy statement applies to that processing. Nothing is sent from the app to this website.
