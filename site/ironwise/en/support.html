Help for Ironwise

Ironwise is for you who train with weights: log your sets quickly, see your records and progress, and follow your friends' workouts. The app keeps track of your records, streaks and programs.

Contact

Describe the problem and, if you can, include your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version. Only send screenshots without private information. We respond to reports of content and users within 24 hours.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find my friends?

Tap Friends at the top of Feed (or under You) and search for your friend's username. Send a request. When your friend accepts, you see each other's workouts in Feed.

How do I report or block a user or a comment?

Tap the three dots next to the user or the comment and choose Report or Block. When you block, the friendship is removed and you no longer see each other's content. You can undo a block under Friends. We handle reports within 24 hours.

I cannot log sets without internet

You can log without internet. The sets are saved on the phone and sent automatically when you have a connection again. The top shows how many changes are waiting.

Why does the app show a different weight from what I typed?

The app always stores weight in kg. If you switch to lbs under You, it is converted, and there can be small roundings.

I got a new phone

Sign in with the same Apple or Google account and the app fetches all your exercises, sets and programs from the server.

My Apple or Google sign-in does not work

Under You you can connect both Apple and Google to the same profile so you can always get in. If you have lost access to both, write to support.

Delete my data or my account

Under You in the app you can delete all your training history or choose "Delete my account". Your profile, exercises, sets, programs, friends, comments and sign-in are then deleted immediately, and your Apple sign-in is revoked. This cannot be undone. If you cannot get into the app, write to kristoffer@bundgaards.net and we will help you. See also the privacy policy.
