Privacy policy

Packday is published by Kristoffer Bjerregaard Bundgaard.

Your trips and packing lists are stored locally on your device. Packday does not send them to a server operated by us.

Last updated: 5 October 2026 · Applies to Packday 1.0.

What the app stores

Packday stores trip names, trip type, departure and return dates, packing lists with names, categories, quantities and packing status, archived trips, your saved starter lists and kits, chosen reminder times and language choice. This is used to display and edit your lists and schedule your local reminders. The contents are saved in the app's local storage.

The app reads your phone's language setting to select Danish or English on first use. It does not request access to your location, contacts, photos or camera.

Accounts and server data

Packday has no user account, login or server database for your trips. We do not receive your trip names, dates or packing lists through the app. The app does not automatically sync between devices.

Purchases

Packday Plus is a one-time purchase through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment and purchase records. The app uses Apple's StoreKit to retrieve the price and verify whether your Apple Account has access to Plus. This purchase information is used to unlock Plus features; the app does not send receipts or transactions to a server operated by us. We do not receive your card details through the app.

Apple's handling of account, payment and store information is described in App Store & Privacy. Your trips are not included in Packday's purchase requests.

Notifications

When you choose a reminder and grant permission, Packday schedules a local notification on your phone. The app does not register a push token or send notifications from a server. The notification's visible text does not include your trip name or packing list contents.

You can remove a reminder from the trip's menu and turn off notifications in your iPhone settings. Reminders are removed when a trip is finished or deleted, or all items are packed.

Backup files and sharing

If you choose “Export trips”, the app creates a JSON file containing your trips, lists, reminder times and language choice, then opens your phone's sharing function. You decide whether to save or send the file. It does not include your Apple purchase. Recipients and storage services you choose handle the file under their own terms and privacy policies. Your phone's own backups may also include app data depending on your settings.

When you choose “Import trips”, Packday reads a file you select locally and replaces the app's current contents after your confirmation. Imported reminders are turned off. Copies stored outside the app are not changed or deleted by Packday.

Advertising and tracking

Packday has no advertising, analytics, behaviour tracking or AI service. The app does not use a separate service to send crash reports or download app code outside the App Store.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can view and edit your trips and packing lists in the app. You can export a backup, delete individual trips or choose Settings → “Delete all local data”. This deletes trips, saved starter lists and kits from the app on the device and does not affect your Apple purchase. Your language choice is preserved. Data you exported, shared or saved in another service must be deleted there.

Contacting support

If you email support, we receive your email address and the contents you choose to send so we can respond to your request. This is separate from the app's local storage. Do not send passwords, card details or a backup containing private trip information. Contact kristoffer@bundgaards.net with privacy questions or to request access to, correction of or deletion of your correspondence.

Visiting this website

These pages are static. We have not added contact forms, cookies, advertising, analytics or external fonts. The pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address, browser details, time of visit and the page requested to deliver and protect the service. GitHub's privacy statement describes this processing. Packday does not send your trips or packing lists to this website.
