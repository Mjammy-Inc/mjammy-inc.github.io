Help with Packday

A little less to remember before departure. Packday brings your trip and packing list together, with a separate “Pack last” section for the things you use until the last minute.

Your trips are stored on your iPhone. You do not need an account to use the app.

Common questions

How do I get started?

Choose “Plan your first trip” or “New trip”, give it a name, and select your departure and return dates. Choose a suggested starter list or start empty, then tap “Create packing list”. You can then add, edit and mark items as packed.

What is “Pack last”?

Open an item on your packing list and turn on “Pack last”. It gets its own place, so you can keep track of things such as your phone, charger and toothbrush that you use right before departure.

What is free, and what does Plus add?

The free version includes one active trip at a time, built-in starter lists, “Pack last” and local reminders. Finish a trip to make room for the next one; the finished trip remains in your trip history. Packday Plus is a one-time purchase for more active trips, reusing trips, your own starter lists and packing kits. Both versions support up to 500 items per list. Apple displays the current price in the app before purchase. Plus is not a subscription.

How do I restore Plus?

Use the Apple Account you purchased Plus with, then choose “Restore purchases” in Settings. Connecting to Apple's store may require internet and approval from Apple. Restoring purchases restores access to Plus, not your trips or packing lists. If it fails, try again or send the displayed error code to support.

Why did my reminder not appear?

Open your trip's menu and choose “One last check” to set a future date and time. Allow notifications for Packday in your iPhone settings. The reminder is scheduled locally and is used only while the trip is active and something remains unpacked. Your iPhone notification and Focus settings can affect when it appears. You can remove the reminder in the app or turn off notifications in your iPhone settings.

How do I move my trips to a new phone?

Choose Settings → “Export trips” and save the backup file somewhere you choose. On your new phone, choose “Import trips”. Import replaces the trips and saved lists already in the app and turns off imported reminders. Export first if you want to keep the existing contents too. The backup includes your trips, saved lists and language choice, but not your Apple purchase. Packday does not automatically sync between phones.

A change has not been saved – what should I do?

If the app shows a save error, keep it open and choose “Retry”. An error while opening the app means Packday could not read the saved trips; it does not replace them with an empty list. Avoid deleting the app before you have secured a backup of your trips.

Delete your data

Choose Settings → “Delete all local data” and confirm. This deletes the app's trips, starter lists and kits on this device. You can also delete an individual trip from its menu. There is no Packday account to delete, and your Apple purchase is unaffected. Backup files you saved or shared must be deleted wherever they are stored.
